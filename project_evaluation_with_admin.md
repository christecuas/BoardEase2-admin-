# Project Evaluation

This study focused on developing the BoardEase: A Mobile App for Smart Boarding House Management, a system designed to Improve how boarding house owners and boarders manage daily operations, communication, and rental processes. The application aimed to address the limitations of traditional paper-based or manual management methods, which often led to inconsistent records, delayed communication, and difficulties in tracking rent payments and applications. These concerns served as the foundation for identifying the system's essential features and overall functionality.

The initial phase of the system's development involved creating an interface for boarders. This interface allowed users to browse available boarding houses, view room details, check availability, and access essential information such as rental rates, house rules, location, and owner contact details. The design prioritized usability to ensure that boarders could easily explore options and make informed decisions based on the details provided.

The next phase centered on implementing the application and rental features. Boarders were given the ability to submit room applications directly through the app, which were then subject to owner review and validation. This process ensured that applications were screened appropriately and that boarders could proceed only after meeting the necessary requirements. This replaced the typical walk-in or text-based inquiries that often created confusion or delays.

To enhance communication and security, the system incorporated several supporting features. A built-in messaging component enabled real-time interaction between boarders and owners, allowing them to clarify concerns, discuss room rules, or address maintenance-related issues without relying on external platforms. Additionally, the system introduced secure account handling and verification procedures to protect user data and ensure that only authorized individuals could access their accounts and records.

The final stage focused on developing the admin interface for boarding house owners. This interface provided tools for managing room listings, monitoring boarder applications, handling rent records, sending announcements or reminders, and overseeing maintenance requests. Owners were also able to update boarding house information and track active tenants more efficiently. These tools allowed owners to streamline management processes, reducing manual workload and minimizing record-keeping errors.

Beyond the owner interface, the system also incorporated a comprehensive administrative and superadmin panel designed to provide platform-wide oversight and management capabilities. The superadmin interface serves as the central control system for monitoring and managing the entire BoardEase platform. Super administrators are equipped with powerful tools to oversee all aspects of the system, including user management, registration approvals, boarding house oversight, and comprehensive analytics.

The superadmin panel enables administrators to review and approve or reject pending user registrations, ensuring that only verified and legitimate users gain access to the platform. Administrators can manage all user accounts, including the ability to view user details, suspend or activate accounts, and monitor user activity across both boarder and owner roles. This centralized user management system helps maintain platform integrity and security.

Additionally, the superadmin interface provides comprehensive analytics and reporting capabilities. Administrators can access detailed statistics on user growth, boarding house performance, booking trends, payment and revenue analytics, occupancy rates, and geographic distribution of users and properties. The system tracks growth trends over time, identifies top-performing boarding houses, and monitors revenue generation across the platform. These analytics tools enable data-driven decision-making and help identify areas for platform improvement.

The superadmin panel also includes dispute management features, allowing administrators to review and resolve conflicts between users, monitor flagged accounts with multiple issues, and take appropriate actions to maintain a safe and trustworthy platform environment. All administrative activities are logged with detailed information including IP addresses and user agents, providing a complete audit trail for security and accountability purposes.

By integrating these features, BoardEase provided a more organized, accessible, and transparent system for both boarders and boarding house owners. The application improved the overall management workflow, simplified tenant-related transactions, and strengthened communication—ultimately creating a modernized approach to boarding house operation management. The addition of the superadmin panel further enhanced the platform's reliability and scalability by providing centralized oversight and comprehensive management tools that ensure the system operates smoothly and securely at scale.
